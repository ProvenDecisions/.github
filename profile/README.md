## ProvenDecisions: optimization answers you can check

**[ProvenDecisions](https://proven-decisions.ai/?ref=github)** solves optimization problems (linear, integer,
quadratic, convex and more) from typed math, LaTeX, a photo or a spoken question, and **proves** every answer: each
constraint is checked, and a dual or KKT certificate shows that nothing better exists. Every number comes from a real
solver (HiGHS, Clarabel, SCIP via cvxpy), never from a language model.

### 📓 Free notebooks

**[optimization-examples](https://github.com/ProvenDecisions/optimization-examples)**: classic optimization problems as
Jupyter notebooks (the problem, the model with a note on every line, cvxpy code and its real output, the checked
answer). Open any of them in Google Colab and press Run. New notebooks, tutorials and a monthly digest of what is new
in optimization are added every month: ⭐ star or 👀 watch the repository to hear about them.
